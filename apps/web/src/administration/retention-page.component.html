Data retention

Organization administrators choose how long preview runs, regression runs, conversations and invocation history are kept. There are no defaults: a family that isn't configured keeps its data. Audit and AI usage records keep their own fixed retention and can't be shortened here.

A policy applies to data created after you save it unless you explicitly apply it to existing data. Cleanup runs in the background and permanently deletes data once its period has passed; due conversations are hidden right away. Regression runs that ended before this feature have no reliable end time and are kept.
