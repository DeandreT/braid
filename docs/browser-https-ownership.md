# Qualification admission and original-operation closure

The nonshipped sole Fetch adapter admits exact product operation headers and fresh
purpose-bound AF values. Duplicate/control/value and route/method roles remain closed;
product AF/OIDC/cookie authority is unchanged. Conditional grants require actual final-
wire equality; Cookie and every sec-fetch override are omitted. Each actual pause event
owns one terminal command, with synchronous claim and original-command settlement.

Probe launches bind immutable generation, original child and once-read closed report
independently of readiness/counts admission. Baselines are zero and refusal totals archive
every confirmed generation. Ordinary cases require zero host/proxy refusals; literal
named vectors have exact finite allowances. Unknown original work retains dependencies.

The private control server owns its original accept loop, workers, handlers and writes.
After closing intake/listener it joins accepting before snapshotting/joining workers.
Only after those originals and local channel cleanup does it publish ControlSettled.
Its passing Settled flag and CloseAsync validation still require zero request/delivery
failures. A joined failed response write is known failure, not an unjoined task.

The parent observes that same memoized control close, records control failure and requires
explicit positive settlement separately from error categorization. After original browser,
tool/output and proxy/probe owners are also confirmed, the original dependent cleanup may
run even if a final reply was never delivered. No reply is retried or promoted to success.
Primary, counter, control and secondary cleanup failures remain independent overall
failures. A cancellation, timeout or caught exception alone never authorizes release.

Network close and memoized case drain remain independently observed. Ordered first
pool/DB/ring/NSS/private-file release remains single-attempt and requires confirmed owners.
The bounded claim covers HTTP(S)/WS(S) destination/product delivery in the trusted owned
scenario, not arbitrary clients or a UDP/WebRTC/DNS/QUIC/OS packet sandbox.
