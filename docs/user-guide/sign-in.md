Weft runs in one of two ways, chosen by whoever runs it:

- A **signed-in workspace** uses your Microsoft work account.
- A **Development workspace** runs on a developer's own computer. It has no
  sign-in: you are already identified, and the header shows
  **Development workspace**.

## Sign in

1. Open Weft in your browser.
2. In the **Session** panel near the top of the page, choose **Sign in**.
3. Sign in with your work account on the Microsoft page.
4. Microsoft returns you to Weft, and the panel shows **Signed in to Weft**.

Weft opens your organization for you. There is no organization to pick.

Signing in leaves the page and reloads it. Anything you haven't saved, such as
a form you were filling in, is lost, so finish or save your work first. A change
you already sent may have been saved even if you didn't see the reply.

## Sign out

Choose **Sign out** in the **Session** panel. This ends your Weft session in
this browser. It doesn't sign you out of your Microsoft account or other
Microsoft services.

**Sign in again** repeats the Microsoft sign-in while you are signed in. It
replaces your current Weft session with a new one.

## When something goes wrong

- **Sign-in could not be completed**: Microsoft signed you in, but Weft didn't
  accept the account. Your account may not be set up for Weft yet. Ask your Weft
  administrator to add it, then choose **Return to Weft** and sign in again.
- **Sign-in is temporarily unavailable**: Weft couldn't finish sign-in. Try
  again later.
- **Sign-in required**: your earlier session can no longer be used. Choose
  **Sign in**.
- **Sign-in status unavailable**: Weft couldn't check your session, so it hides
  your workspace until it can. This doesn't mean you were signed out. Choose
  **Check session** to try again.

Signing in only proves who you are. What you can see and change depends on your
project roles, which Weft checks again for every action. See
[Projects and roles](catalog.md#projects-and-roles).
